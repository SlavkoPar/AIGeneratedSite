A simple, solid page layout

This site is built around three fixed parts: a header across the top, a navigation bar down the left side, and a footer at the bottom. The content you are reading sits in the middle.

See the features About the layout

The three parts

Header

Stays at the top of the screen while you scroll and holds the site name. On small screens it also holds the menu button.

Left navigation

A vertical list of links that stays in view on wide screens and slides in as a drawer on phones and tablets.

Footer

Sits at the bottom of every page, even when the page content is short, with secondary links and a copyright line.
