FAQ

Questions? Answers.

Is there really a free plan?

Yes. The Starter plan is free forever for up to 3 projects. Paid plans also include a 14-day free trial.

Can I import data from other tools?

Absolutely. We support one-click imports from Jira, Asana, Trello, Linear, Notion and CSV.

How does billing work?

You’re billed per active user, monthly or yearly. Add or remove seats at any time and we’ll prorate the difference.

Is my data secure?

Lumen is SOC 2 Type II certified, encrypts data at rest and in transit, and offers SSO on Enterprise plans.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes, there are no long-term contracts. Cancel from your settings in two clicks.
